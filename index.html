Projects

Go-Kart Design & Fabrication

Part of the braking system design team in the go-kart project. We designed and analyzed a hydraulic double braking system to improve safety and reliability. The system ensures that if one brake circuit fails, the second braking mechanism continues to operate, maintaining control of the kart. The design focused on braking efficiency, safety, and performance during high-speed operation.



Smart Non-Invasive Load Monitoring System for Cranes

Designed a non-invasive IoT-based system to measure crane load using sensor data without modifying the crane structure. The system includes an automatic logging feature that records load values, time, and location data to improve operational safety and monitoring. This approach enables real-time load tracking while maintaining the structural integrity of the crane.



Design and Development of a Low-Cost Moisture-Proof Grain Storage Silo for Small Farmers

Designed and assembled an agricultural grain storage silo for a TANCAM project team. The silo was developed to provide safe, low-cost, and pest-resistant grain storage for small and marginal farmers. The design focused on moisture protection, structural reliability, thermal insulation, and efficient grain discharge using industry-feasible materials and manufacturing methods. The project team received a Special Prize for their design, and I was responsible for the silo design and assembly.



V6 Engine Design – Junior Design Engineer Course

Designed a complete V6 internal combustion engine as part of the Junior Design Engineer course. The project involved developing the engine layout, cylinder configuration, crankshaft arrangement, and major engine components using CAD design principles. The design focused on proper component integration, mechanical functionality, and understanding of engine architecture used in modern automotive systems.
